Der, die, das

Six rules that save the most guesses

Plural

Every plural is die

Der Mann becomes die Männer and das Kind becomes die Kinder. Gender only matters in the singular.

Compounds

The last word decides

Der Handschuh takes Schuh. Die Haustür takes Tür. Das Wörterbuch takes Buch.

Diminutives

-chen and -lein are always das

Grammar beats biology. Das Mädchen and das Fräulein are neuter, even though they mean a girl and a young lady.

Verbs and adjectives

Turned into a noun, it is das

Das Essen (eating, food), das Leben (life), das Schöne (the beautiful). Any word made into a noun is neuter.

Final -e

Ends in -e? Probably die

About nine in ten are feminine: die Lampe, die Tasche. Exceptions are mostly male people and animals: der Junge, der Löwe, der Affe.

Loanwords

Latin and Greek endings are predictable

-ion, -tät, -ik, -ur, -ie are die. -ment, -um, -ma are das. -or, -ist, -ant, -us are der.
